Compensation scheme 金融服务补偿计划
We are covered by the Financial Services Compensation Scheme (FSCS). The FSCS can pay compensation to depositors if a bank is unable to meet its financial obligations. Most depositors, including
individuals, corporates (regardless of size, unless falling under any another exclusion) and small local authorities are covered by the scheme.
我们受金融服务补偿计划（FSCS）的保护。在银行无法履行其金融职责时， FSCS可以向存款客户提供补偿。绝大多数个人、公司企业（不论规模大小除非另有规定）及小型地方性政府机
构的存款客户均受此计划的保护。
For more information on the FSCS including information on deposits excluded from protection under the scheme, please refer to the Information Sheet and Exclusions List.
关于金融服务补偿计划（FSCS）不受保护存款的更多详情，请参见信息和排除表。
In respect of deposits, an eligible depositor is entitled to claim up to £85,000. For joint accounts, each account holder is treated as having a claim in respect of their share so, for a joint account held by two
eligible depositors, the maximum amount that could be claimed would be £85,000 each (making a total of £170,000). The £85,000 limit relates to the combined amount in all the eligible depositor’s accounts
with the Bank, including their share of any joint account and not to each separate account.
对于存款，符合条件的存款客户最高可以获得限额为8.5万英镑的补偿。联名账户的每一位账户持有人按其所拥有的份额获得补偿。如由两个存款客户共同拥有的联名账户，每一位持有人的
最高补偿限额为8.5万英镑 （总额最高为17万英镑）。8.5万英镑的限额适用于符合条件的存款客户在同一银行所持有的所有账户的存款总额，包括其拥有的联名账户份额，而并非每一个独立
的账户均可得到上述限额补偿。
